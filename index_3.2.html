University of Dundee Marketing Society

Year 1

I joined the UoD Marketing Society Committee as Treasurer at the end of my 2nd year. We had a difficult task ahead of us going into 24/25, reviving a society that had died during the pandemic and bring it to the same level as the established societies by the end of the year. I was looking forward to this as it would be a chance to put my theory to the test and handle real sums of money and use it to effectively grow an organisation.

So far into our 2nd semester the society has growing well, the events we have done and are scheduled to do have put us at the top in terms of being able to offer students valuable experience in their industry. For example, we will soon be hosting Guy Lomas, Head of Marketing and Global Events for Phillips, who will be giving us some great insight with his wealth of experience and a masterclass on sales, an applicable skill to any student in this day and age.

While the committee has sought to successfully grow the society within the university. I have taken a more macro approach. I have been leveraging the unique benefits of a society to create a positive feedback loop for it. In other words, I have been connecting with businesses and organisations outside the university by offering partnership. For example, I have partnered with an Airbnb owner in Turin, Italy for a marketing event, where students compete to create a marketing campaign for the Airbnb, developing their portfolios, for the prize of a 5 night stay in the rustic property. This boosts our society's reach, and value, while empowering our members, and supporting businesses both in the local area, and beyond. We are already seeing benefits as more businesses have seen our proof of concept and are interested in partnering for events.

Jude, the society's President and amazing mentor, took notice of my ambition to grow the society. He gave me his vote of confidence by promoting me to accounts manager and giving me full autonomy in pursuing these avenues of growth. Moreover, recognising my interest in the society's future he has backed me for President next year, which I have accepted.

I am very excited to see how far we go this semester, and next year.
