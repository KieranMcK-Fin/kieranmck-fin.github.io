University of Dundee Finance Society

I decided to become a member of the UoD Finance Society shortly after it was created in my 3rd year. I knew that it would be a great opportunity to network, and learn from both my coursemates and guest lecturers. The events have been very interesting, especially for a new society, and I can definitely say that I've made some good friends and strengthened the connections I already had from my course. Moreover, the other members have motivated me to keep applying myself to my course.
