Analyst of the UoD Student Managed Fund

Shortly after the inception of the UoD Finance Society there was a spin off in the shape of a Student Managed Fund. When I learned about this I was extremely keen to join, as being a member of a SMF is a great opportunity to develop analytical, risk management, software proficiency and security analysis skills. Moreover, it's the closest most students will get to experience in the financial markets outside of an internship. And while it has become more accessible in recent years, it's still quite niche and something only a handful of students on a finance course will be able to say they've done. All this evidences how valuable it is to have a SMF membership on your CV.

However, I was unfortunately rejected as I had learned about it (and thus applied for it) too late in the year, and the team for that year had been picked. Despite that, only a couple of weeks later as I had gotten to know a lot of the members through our shared membership in the Finance Society and our course, on top of spending a lot of time with them in the Bloomberg Terminal Lab, they saw my work ethic and what I could bring to the fund. And so Fergus, the Portfolio Manager, invited me into the fund as an Analyst.

So far the experience has been great, with each analyst being given a week to pick a stock and build a pitchdeck to present to the Fund Manager and the rest of the team. I like this as we build our teamworking skills, but there's still an emphasis on individuality and competition.

As the fund is new and the first of its kind in the University of Dundee, we are working with simulated funds on the Bloomberg Terminal. Although once we develop a strong track record, we will be reaching out for funding. I believe my ambitious attitude and experience in the Marketing Society will empower me to take a leading position in this future for the fund. I think skills like these are the making of any effective future Portfolio Manager.
