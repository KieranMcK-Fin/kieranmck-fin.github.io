LSEG Refinitv

After getting exposure to the Bloomberg Terminal, I was introduced to LSEG Refinitiv by a course professor and given an account earlier than anyone else in my course. Unlike the Terminal, Refinitiv is available to personal computers, this has given me the ability to perform technical analysis from anywhere, even while away from campus. This accessibility has motivated me to spend more time researching both stocks/indices on top of macroeconomic trends.

Moreover, my account grants me access to LSEG hosted webinars on a wide range of topics from FX, Commodity trading, technical analysis and more. This has allowed me to gain valuable experience and hone in on specific topics while developing connections with industry professionals.
